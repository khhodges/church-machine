Lump Size Rule

Lump size is always a power of 2, minimum 64 words, maximum 32 768 words (32-bit each):

lumpSize = 2^n where 6 ≤ n ≤ 15 freespace = lumpSize - 1 - cw - cc (must be all-zero; Mint verifies at load time)

The maximum is 215 = 32 768 words. The header cw field (13 bits, max 8 191) and cc field (8 bits, max 255) together cap the maximum useful payload at 1 + 8 191 + 255 = 8 447 words. Mint hard-rejects n‑6 > 9 (lumpSize > 32 K).
