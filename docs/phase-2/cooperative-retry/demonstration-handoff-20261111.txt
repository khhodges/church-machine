WARNINGS AND BOUNDARIES
- The build log retains 100 Netlist 29-72 critical warnings about INIT
  properties. Direct inspection of the exact routed checkpoint confirmed all
  24 SHA state words / 768 bits match the required initialization constants.
  This addresses the specific suspected state-initialization loss, not every
  possible synthesis concern. The diagnostic reports are included unchanged.
- CFGBVS-1 remains in the original BIT's evidence. The manufacturer's V3
  schematic shows VCCO_0 and CFGBVS_0 on 3V3, supporting CFGBVS=VCCO and
  CONFIG_VOLTAGE=3.3. Those declarations were omitted from this experimental
  constraint file. No source correction or replacement build was made here.
- The vendor receipt intentionally still says physicalTest=false and
  programmingAuthorized=false: it records the earlier vendor-only build.
  It has not been rewritten to incorporate later operator trials.
- The initial attempt used configuration reset and restored the stored
  earlier build. The combined BIT was subsequently reloaded. The successful
  application upload, old-LUMP delivery, adoption and replacement followed
  without a further reported FPGA reload.
- KEY1 is SW3 (M6), not SW1. SW1 is PROG_B/configuration reset.
- The JTAG cable identifier is not the physical board's silicon identity.
  Board selection was operator-confirmed; no live DNA attestation or BIT
  readback is included in these UART reports.
- No hardware proof is claimed here for stale-epoch rejection, malformed/
  wrong-phase LUMP rejection, LUMP loading while USER B progresses, long
  endurance, power-cycle persistence, or boot-flash installation.
- The physical replacement direction was All Waiting -> USER B. The older
  local RTL sequence exercised USER B -> All Waiting. Do not conflate them.
