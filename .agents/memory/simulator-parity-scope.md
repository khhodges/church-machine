name: Simulator parity investment boundary
description: Prefer bounded exact-image diagnosis over making comprehensive simulator parity a hardware prerequisite.
---

Use a bounded one-image investigation before proposing simulator/FPGA alignment
work. Reuse existing loading facilities, identify the first incompatibility and
recommend one narrow correction. Do not expand automatically into full parity,
new infrastructure or the complete SYSTEM/IDE Lazy lifecycle.

**Why:** The user rejected the broad alignment proposal as high-risk and expensive
and accepted replacing it with “minimal risk for maximum progress.” Hardware-first
acceptance remains authoritative.

**How to apply:** Treat major execution-model gaps as a stop/reconsider decision,
not implicit implementation approval. Plan revision, investigation, corrections
and physical operations have separate authorization boundaries.
