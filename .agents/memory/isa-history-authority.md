---
name: ISA historical evidence authority
description: Historical deduplication must not promote a unique historical paragraph into normative guidance.
---
Preserving a unique historical paragraph does not make it a current normative
decision, even when a consistency test previously expected that text in the master.

**Why:** The master explicitly separated current corrections from historical
baselines before the repeated material was extracted. The two historical RETURN
versions differ, but their uniqueness alone supplies no authority to choose one.

**How to apply:** Keep historical variants as evidence and preserve their
provenance. Change ISA authority only with an explicit decision, never by inferring
it from implementation behavior or from an outdated test's file location.
However, a historical wrapper may encompass a still-current contract: check
explicit cross-document authority before extraction. The SWITCH lifecycle
specification and its existing documentation gate establish that its concise
definition belongs in the master. Preserve such established current contracts
separately without reinstating obsolete neighboring text.
