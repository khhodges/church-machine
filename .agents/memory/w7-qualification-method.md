---
name: W7 qualification methodology
description: Owner review requires complete mediation and correct grants as separate evidence obligations.
---

Treat complete mediation and correct initial/dynamic grants as co-equal parts of
the security case. A reviewed ingress-to-sink route inventory must justify its
own completeness, including debug/readback, dormant FSMs, M6, UART and privileged
interfaces. Begin check-to-use reconciliation across Cleaner, Amobea, reload,
GC/reuse, Factory Build and the three-Thread scenario.

**Why:** The owner's review emphasized that tests alone cannot establish route
coverage, and perfectly enforced excessive grants still defeat confinement.

**How to apply:** Fix claim nature and phase before results; separate them from
execution status. Required claims cannot become assumptions or limitations to
hide failures. Bind physical conclusions to verified-installed data bytes as
well as distinct BIT/device identity evidence. Fault recovery is not one-shot
checkpoint security. Review methodology does not authorize hardware changes.
