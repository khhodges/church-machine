description: User-defined separation of small-screen header controls.
---

The small-screen left stack ends at Download diagnostics. Execution controls
belong in a separate central group; Wukong status and Settings form the right stack.

**Why:** The user corrected a layout that put execution controls beneath the
left stack; wrapping all controls together does not meet the intended grouping.

**How to apply:** Preserve these three independent groups when adjusting
responsive header layouts, even when central controls must wrap.
