---
name: Offline evidence licensing decision
description: Owner-confirmed scope and ownership for offline diagnostic redistribution
---

The owner personally claims all rights and explicitly approved GPL-3.0 for
the entire bounded offline evidence package, including parser/tooling and
historical capture, event logs and reports, with optional commercial licensing
for permissions outside GPL. Do not attribute ownership to a company.

**Why:** The initial proposed policy required licensing for all commercial
use; after clarification, the owner selected GPL plus optional commercial
licensing and expressly included the historical evidence.

**How to apply:** Preserve this bounded scope rather than inferring a
repository-wide grant. Vendor binaries and unrelated uploads stay excluded.
Recording rights is not authorization to publish or alter historical evidence.
Patent applications were owner-reported, not independently verified.
